Key findings
  • 1470 students have fully dropped since 2024. The top five reasons — Busy (246, 17%), Break (237, 16%), Budget (227, 15%), Attendance (181, 12%), Lost Interest (115, 8%) — account for 68% of all drops. A further 203 (14%) are uncategorized — a drop-form data-hygiene gap worth closing.
  • Different reasons need different plays: Break / Busy / Schedule drops are often recoverable (a pause or a timing clash) — the Break Re-engagement queue targets these. Budget, Lost Interest, and Medical are harder to win back and point more to fit, pricing, or value than to scheduling.
  • Peak quarter was 2026-Q3 (203 drops); the current quarter (2026-Q4) sits at 0 so far.
  • Growth vs. churn: over the last 12 months, 959 started and 555 dropped — a net of +404, but churn erased 58% of gross new starts. (Full month-by-month trend lives on Brad's CRO Retention page.)
