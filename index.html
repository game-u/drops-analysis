Key findings
  • 1316 students have fully dropped since 2024. The top five reasons — Break (236, 18%), Busy (223, 17%), Budget (187, 14%), Attendance (167, 13%), Lost Interest (106, 8%) — account for 70% of all drops. A further 168 (13%) are uncategorized — a drop-form data-hygiene gap worth closing.
  • Different reasons need different plays: Break / Busy / Schedule drops are often recoverable (a pause or a timing clash) — the Break Re-engagement queue targets these. Budget, Lost Interest, and Medical are harder to win back and point more to fit, pricing, or value than to scheduling.
  • Peak quarter was 2024-Q2 (194 drops); the current quarter (2026-Q3) sits at 38 so far.
